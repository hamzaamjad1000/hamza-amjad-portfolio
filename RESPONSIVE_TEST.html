📋 Responsive Design Checklist

💡 Testing Tips:
1. Use your browser's device emulation (F12 → Toggle device toolbar)
2. Test on iPhone SE (375px), iPhone 12 (390px), Samsung S21 (360px)
3. Test on iPad (768px) and iPad Pro (1024px)
4. Rotate your device and check landscape mode
5. Verify all content is visible without zooming
6. Check that no elements overflow horizontally
